# Research Paper Collection

A curated collection of verified scholarly papers related to AI-powered literature discovery, semantic scholarly search, citation networks, systematic literature reviews, and AI-assisted research.

## 1. Survey and Review Papers

### 1. Artificial Intelligence in Literature Review Synthesis: A Step-by-Step Methodological Approach for Researchers and Academics

Dr. Matolwandile Mzuvukile Mtotywa, 2026, *Informatics*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/informatics13030043)

Provides a structured approach for using AI in literature-review synthesis and is directly relevant to AI-assisted research workflows.

### 2. Artificial Intelligence Tools in Biomedical Research: Part 1—Literature Search and Knowledge Mining

Sen, 2026, *Antioxidants & Redox Signaling*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/15230864251405885)

Reviews AI tools for biomedical literature searching and knowledge mining, supporting the cross-disciplinary focus of this project.

### 3. Developing a Critical Imagination for How Researchers Can Use Artificially Intelligent Tools Reflexively and Responsibly During Qualitative Literature Reviews

Foley et al., 2025, *International Journal of Qualitative Methods*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/16094069251316249)

Examines responsible and critical use of AI during literature reviews, highlighting the importance of human judgment.

### 4. Our AI-Powered Discoveries Are Trapped in a Predigital System

Chew, 2026, *Journal of Medical Internet Research*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.2196/96018)

Discusses challenges associated with AI-powered academic discovery and the infrastructure surrounding scholarly communication.

## 2. Foundational Papers

### 5. Construction of the Literature Graph in Semantic Scholar

Ammar et al., 2018, *NAACL-HLT*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/N18-3011)

Introduces the literature graph underlying Semantic Scholar and is foundational to AI-powered scholarly discovery.

### 6. S2ORC: The Semantic Scholar Open Research Corpus

Lo et al., 2020, *ACL*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.447)

Introduces a large open scholarly corpus containing papers, references, abstracts, and structured full text for research.

### 7. SPECTER: Document-level Representation Learning using Citation-informed Transformers

Cohan et al., 2020, *ACL*
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.447)

Presents a citation-informed document representation method useful for scientific-paper similarity and retrieval.

### 8. OpenAlex: A Fully-Open Index of Scholarly Works, Authors, Venues, Institutions, and Concepts

Priem et al., 2022
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01833)

Introduces OpenAlex, an open scholarly knowledge graph useful for large-scale literature and citation analysis.
