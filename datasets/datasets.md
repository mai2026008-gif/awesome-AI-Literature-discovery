# Datasets

## 1. OpenAlex

**Source:** OurResearch

[Dataset / Website](https://openalex.org/)

OpenAlex is an open scholarly database containing information about research papers, authors, journals, institutions, and citations. It can be used for academic literature discovery, citation analysis, and building scholarly search systems.

**Use:** Literature search, citation analysis, paper recommendation, and scholarly knowledge-graph research.

---

## 2. S2ORC – Semantic Scholar Open Research Corpus

**Source:** Allen Institute for AI

[Dataset / GitHub](https://github.com/allenai/s2orc)

S2ORC is a large scholarly corpus containing research papers, abstracts, citations, references, and structured full-text information. It is useful for developing and evaluating systems that process and search academic literature.

**Use:** Natural language processing, semantic search, paper similarity, citation analysis, and literature mining.

---

## 3. SYNERGY Dataset

**Source:** ASReview

[Dataset / GitHub](https://github.com/asreview/synergy-dataset)

SYNERGY is a dataset created from systematic literature reviews and contains academic records with relevance labels. It can be used to evaluate machine-learning and information-retrieval methods for identifying relevant research papers.

**Use:** Literature screening, information retrieval, systematic reviews, and evaluation of AI-assisted literature discovery.


## Future Dataset Directions

- Develop larger datasets for AI-powered literature discovery.
- Include research papers from multiple academic disciplines.
- Add relevance judgments to identify useful papers.
- Include citation and reference relationships between papers.
- Add expert evaluations of paper relevance and quality.
- Include publication dates and research areas.
- Create datasets for comparing different literature discovery tools.
- Support evaluation of relevance, coverage, novel discovery, efficiency, and transparency.
- Improve the accuracy and reproducibility of AI-assisted literature discovery research.
