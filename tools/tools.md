1. ResearchRabbit — Find papers and research connections

Use ResearchRabbit when you have a few papers already and want to discover more papers related to them.

Typical workflow:

Start with 2–5 important papers → add them to ResearchRabbit → explore related papers → explore authors → identify older/newer papers → save relevant papers.

2. Elicit — Understand and extract information from papers

After collecting papers, use Elicit to make the literature review easier.

3. Litmaps — Map the literature

Litmaps is particularly useful when you want to understand the structure and evolution of research in your topic.

You can start with a relevant paper and build a map of related research.

4. PRISMA — This one is different

PRISMA is not really a literature-search tool like the other three.

PRISMA is a framework/guideline for conducting and reporting a systematic review.
